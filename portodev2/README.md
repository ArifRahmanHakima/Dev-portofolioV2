# 🚀 Hindina — Modern Developer Portfolio Template

![Preview](img/preview.png)

A sleek, fully responsive personal portfolio template for developers, AI engineers, and UI/UX designers.  
Built to showcase your professional journey, skills, and projects with a modern, animated design.

🔗 **Live Demo**: [https://hindina-porto.netlify.app/]

---

## ✨ Features

- ⚡ **Hero Section** with animated brain visual
- 🎨 Beautiful gradient typography & buttons
- 🧑‍💻 Role tags (AI Engineer, Frontend Developer, UI/UX Designer)
- 📱 100% Responsive Design (mobile-first)
- 🧭 Navigation: Home, About, Journey, Skills, Projects, Contact
- 🪄 Clean animations with smooth transitions
- 🎯 Call-to-Action buttons: “Get in touch” and “Learn more”
- 🧠 Built for developers who care about design and storytelling

---

## 🖼 Sections Overview

### 🏠 Home
Catchy introduction with animated visual and role highlights.

### 🙋‍♀️ About
A short bio about yourself, goals, and what you're passionate about.

### 🛤 My Journey
A timeline of your education and work experience.

### ⚙️ Skills
List of technical and soft skills with stylish layout.

### 📁 Projects
Showcase your best work with screenshots and links.

### 📬 Contact
Contact form or links to social media & email.
